# User-focused landing refresh

## Goal

Turn both landing-page locales into a clear invitation for an ordinary user to try UltyMyLife in Telegram. Preserve the existing visual language while improving hierarchy, localization, product proof, mobile performance, and conversion.

## Scope

- Update Russian and English landing pages generated by the existing static React bundle.
- Keep current routes, legal pages, Telegram URLs, and product media structure.
- Do not redesign the product itself or add new backend behavior.

## Content direction

All visible marketing copy will speak to the person using the product. It will explain practical outcomes: keeping plans visible, building routines, training consistently, recovering, sleeping better, and seeing progress without switching between apps. Corporate, grant, investor, and product-development language will be removed or reframed wherever it distracts from trying the product.

Russian pages will contain no stray English UI labels. English pages will use natural English copy rather than literal translations.

Primary call to action: open UltyMyLife in Telegram. Secondary call to action: contact the founder where contextually useful. The pitch-deck download button will be removed from the final CTA.

## Hero and navigation

- Move the hero copy higher on desktop and mobile.
- Add visible spacing between the UltyMyLife wordmark and the first navigation item.
- Keep the existing language switch and Mini App CTA.
- Reduce the strength of the ridged/noisy background while retaining a subtle dark texture and color glow.
- Use the supplied English interface images for the English route. Keep Russian product images for the Russian route.

## Feature cards and media modal

All nine cards in the solution section become accessible buttons with hover, focus, and pressed states. Selecting a card opens a modal containing:

- localized section title and short user benefit;
- autoplaying, muted, looping, inline product video;
- poster image for immediate visual feedback;
- localized “Try in Telegram” CTA using the existing Mini App URL;
- close button, Escape-key support, backdrop click, focus management, and body-scroll lock.

Media mapping:

- Habits -> habits video
- Tasks -> tasks video
- Workouts -> training video
- Recovery/Breathing -> recovery video
- Mental fitness -> mental video
- Sleep -> sleep video
- AI assistant -> home video
- Profile -> home video
- Statistics -> home video

If a video cannot play, the poster remains visible and the Telegram CTA remains usable.

## Telegram block

Replace the idealized chat mockup with a cleaner interpretation of the supplied real Telegram screenshot. Include dark patterned chat wallpaper, bot header, introductory message, reminder message, and prominent blue `Open Mini App` control. Copy and surrounding labels are localized. The visual stays illustrative, not a pixel-for-pixel Telegram clone.

## Roadmap and localization fixes

- Move “Standalone website” and “International markets” from growth plans to completed work in both languages.
- Update section descriptions so the roadmap reflects current status.
- Translate “Telegram-native interface” and every other remaining English label on the Russian route.
- Keep genuinely future items under plans.

## Mobile performance

- Remove staggered reveal delays on narrow screens.
- Replace heavy entrance transforms with short opacity-only transitions on mobile.
- Disable decorative parallax and expensive continuous motion on mobile.
- Reduce or remove large backdrop-blur layers on mobile.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling non-essential motion and video autoplay where appropriate.
- Lazy-load offscreen images and videos; modal video loads only when opened.
- Preserve desktop motion where it remains smooth and useful.

## Implementation constraints

The repository contains a production bundle rather than source React modules. Changes will be made conservatively in the existing bundle, localization map, stylesheet, and static assets. Supplied English images will be copied into the product asset directory with stable names. No external dependencies will be introduced.

## Verification

- Run the existing site self-check.
- Verify both `/` and `/ru/` at desktop and phone widths.
- Check navigation spacing, hero placement, background intensity, locale-specific images, and rewritten copy.
- Open every feature modal; verify correct media mapping, close behavior, keyboard behavior, fallback poster, and Telegram CTA.
- Confirm completed/planned roadmap grouping and absence of the pitch-deck button.
- Confirm no untranslated user-facing labels remain on the Russian route.
- Inspect phone scrolling and section reveals for reduced jank.
