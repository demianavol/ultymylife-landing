Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a separate habit tracker app?

No. The tracker opens as a Telegram Mini App, so there is nothing else to install.

Can I track different areas of life?

Yes. Group daily actions around health, growth, productivity and your own priorities.

Will my progress stay connected to other sections?

Yes. Your activity remains part of the same UltyMyLife system alongside the sections you choose to use.

Can I return to this section later?

Yes. Open UltyMyLife in Telegram whenever you want to continue and review your current progress.

Where can I open UltyMyLife?

Open it from the UltyMyLife Telegram bot or any Mini App link.
