Privacy Policy

This is a factual product-data draft, not a final legal policy. Operator identity, jurisdiction, retention periods, legal bases, deletion procedure and processor terms still require confirmation.

Data used by the product

  • Telegram user ID, first name, username, language and avatar URL when Telegram provides them.
  • Profile details entered by the user, including nickname, avatar, age, gender, height, weight, goals, activity level and preferred sections.
  • Habits, tasks, workouts, measurements, sleep, breathing, meditation, recovery and mental-training records.
  • Notification text and schedules.
  • Encrypted cloud-backup content, device identifier and update timestamps.
  • Product analytics such as page views, actions, activity duration, platform, version, timezone offset and session identifiers.
  • AI insight requests, structured progress snapshots, generated reports, usage counts and timestamps.
  • Premium, payment and referral records.
  • Messages sent to the Telegram bot where existing bot handlers process or forward them.

Services involved

Depending on the feature used, data may be processed through Telegram, the UltyMyLife server and database, GitHub Pages, the configured AI provider, YooKassa, Telegram Stars, TON Connect, wallet providers and TON Center.

Local storage

The Mini App uses IndexedDB and localStorage for application data, preferences, analytics delivery, payment return state, referral markers, device identifiers and backup keys.
