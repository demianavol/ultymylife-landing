Personal Data Processing and Privacy Policy

Published and effective: August 9, 2026. This English text is a convenience translation. The Russian version controls.

In short: Data supports features selected by the user. Weight, sleep, well-being, and workout records may be health data and require separate consent. Account access is blocked immediately after a deletion request; application data deletion begins after a 7-day cancellation period. Legally required payment and accounting records may remain longer.

1. Controller

Controller: individual entrepreneur Demian Lyubovevich Avolstiynyi (Авольстийный Демиан Любовьевич), Russian TIN/INN 165049581998, OGRNIP 326169000017372, Russian Federation, Republic of Tatarstan. Data-subject and consent-withdrawal contact: Telegram @demianworkself.

2. Scope

This Policy covers ultymylife.com, the UltyMyLife Telegram Mini App and bot, and related server features. Telegram, payment providers, wallets, and other independent services also apply their own privacy rules.

3. Data categories

  • Telegram/account: ID, name, username, language, avatar URL, Mini App launch verification, sign-in and legal acceptance records.
  • Profile: nickname, avatar, age, sex, height, weight, goals, activity level, selected sections.
  • User records: habits, tasks, workouts, body measurements, sleep, recovery, breathing, meditation, stress, well-being, notes, and exercise results.
  • Potential health data: weight, measurements, sleep, load, recovery, stress, well-being, and related inferences.
  • Notifications, encrypted cloud backup, device ID, versions, timestamps, local keys and settings.
  • Technical use: pages/actions, duration, platform/app/Telegram version, time zone, session, errors, and minimal security metadata.
  • Permitted aggregate numeric indicators for user-requested AI analysis, generated result, usage count, and time.
  • Plan, price, currency/asset, transaction ID/status/provider, Premium status, receipt details supplied to provider, and referral relations.
  • Bot/support messages, rights requests, and request handling history.

Task, habit, and user note content is encrypted on the device before the backup is sent. The Operator does not store the decryption key in its database. User-created names, descriptions, and notes are not sent to the remote AI.

Controller does not request Telegram passwords or codes, full card details, passports, or medical documents. Optional data may be omitted.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Account, synchronization, backup, notifications, support, and selected functions: contract performance and user request; health data also requires separate consent.
  • Personalization, progress analytics, and user-requested AI output: consent.
  • Payments, access, receipts, refunds, tax and accounting: contract and legal obligations.
  • Security, abuse prevention, diagnosis, and legal claims: legitimate interests balanced against user rights and legal-claim grounds.
  • Authority requests: applicable law.

Data is not sold. Unrelated marketing requires a separate legal basis.

5. Processing actions

Controller may collect, record, organize, accumulate, store, update, retrieve, use, compare, transmit to disclosed processors, de-identify, block, delete, and destroy data by automated means and, for support or claims, manually. No biometric identification or solely automated legally significant decisions are performed.

6. Recipients and international processing

  • Telegram for Mini App identity, bot messages, and Stars;
  • UltyMyLife server/database providers for application operations and encrypted backups. The Operator's database does not contain the backup decryption key;
  • GitHub Pages for the public site and ordinary network logs;
  • Groq, LLC (United States) receives only permitted aggregate numeric indicators for AI analysis requested by the user. Task, habit, and exercise names or descriptions, personal reflections, and user notes are not sent. Groq's published rules say ordinary inference content may be temporarily logged for reliability and abuse monitoring for up to 30 days and retained infrastructure is in the United States;
  • YooKassa for ruble payments, refunds, and fiscal data;
  • Telegram Stars; TON Connect, selected wallets, and TON infrastructure. Public blockchain records cannot be erased by Controller.

Groq and some other providers may process the limited data described above outside Russia. A transfer is limited to the feature selected by the user and the named recipient. A new purpose, data category, or recipient requires a separate lawful basis and, where required, fresh consent.

7. Retention

  • Account, profile, records, backup, notifications, and AI material: while the account and selected purpose remain active, then until deletion finishes.
  • Acceptance evidence: while consent applies and afterward as needed to demonstrate lawful processing and defend claims.
  • Security logs: the minimum incident-detection period, longer only for an incident or dispute.
  • Payment, receipt, tax, and accounting records: mandatory statutory period, generally at least five years for the relevant document category.
  • Claims: until resolution and expiry of the applicable claim period.

Data is deleted or de-identified when the purpose ends unless another legal ground requires retention.

8. Withdrawal and deletion

Withdraw consent through @demianworkself and, when available, app settings. Withdrawal does not invalidate prior processing. A confirmed deletion request immediately blocks service processing and may be canceled for 7 calendar days; application data is then deleted or de-identified. Legally retained fiscal, accounting, antifraud, and dispute records are restricted to those purposes. Controller cannot remove public blockchain entries.

9. User rights

A user may request processing information and a copy, correction, blocking, or erasure of unlawful or unnecessary data; withdraw consent; object to legitimate-interest processing; and complain to Controller, Roskomnadzor, or a court. Contact @demianworkself with the request and Telegram ID/username. Controller verifies account control and responds within the statutory period.

10. Security, age, and changes

Measures include access control, Telegram request verification, encrypted transport, log minimization, backup, vulnerability control, and controlled deletion. No method guarantees absolute security. The service is 18+; identified minors’ accounts are blocked and deleted absent a retention duty. A material new purpose, data category, or recipient triggers fresh separate consent.
